MSC THESIS FINDING

The fusion weight

University of Europe for Applied Sciences · Potsdam · 2026

Sparse and dense retrieval are not equal partners — assuming they are costs you accuracy.

Most hybrid RAG implementations split BM25 and dense embedding retrieval 50/50 by default. I ran eleven systematic experiments and found the true optimum at alpha=0.70 — confirmed with a paired t-test (p=0.002). That calibration choice is worth +11.4% Recall@10 over the naive split, on 8.84M passages.

Corpus size8.84M passages
Dense modelMicrosoft E5-base-v2
Sparse methodBM25
Experiments11 tracked (MLflow)
Statistical testPaired t-test
MRR1.0
Latency710ms
Optimal alpha0.70 (p=0.002)
